## Answer

The Market view in front of you has four moving parts I can exercise against right now: the most-asked skills shares, the segment-fit ranking, the audited gaps, and the three new postings the scan recorded today.

Here is a 20-minute exercise that uses all four, in 35-minute writing down to a 20-minute runner.

**Set a 20-minute timer. Build a one-page "segment study card" for the Agent engineer segment.**

**Minute 0 to 5. Pull the levers.**
Open the SKILL DEMAND list. From it, extract the four highest-share skills whose reachable share is equal to or higher than their market share. Those are the levers a Pune candidate can actually move. For the Agent engineer segment those are: Python as the AI application language 71% reachable 71%, Evals and evaluation frameworks 45% reachable 57%, Prototype to production 36% reachable 43%, Agents and agentic systems 28% reachable 29%, plus RAG and retrieval at 22% reachable 43%. Write them at the top of the page with their reachable percentages. Do not write market percentages alone; reachable is what decides what to study next.

**Minute 5 to 12. Map skills to plan rows.**
For each of the four to five skills you wrote, find the single plan row the dashboard flags as the primary cover, and the two supporting rows if there are any. For Agent engineer the primary map is row 53 Agents for the agentic patterns, row 59 Evals as infrastructure for the eval vocabulary, row 28 Production Python architecture for the Python proof, and row 57 Hermes Agent in production for the prototype-to-production muscle. Supporting rows: 51 prompt engineering, 63 drift and feedback loops, 60 observability. Write each row number next to the skill, with its current status word: not started. Do not edit the plan; just annotate the card.

**Minute 12 to 17. Add the gap that lives closest.**

Candidates: Named agent and orchestration frameworks, partly covered at row 53. Voice and speech agents, no plan row. Pick Named frameworks; it is the one you can clear with subtopics of row 53 plus a 2-hour LangGraph side build rather than a whole new row. Write the gap name, the row it touches, and the one-sentence closure ("finish row 53 with CrewAI and AutoGen vocabulary plus a 50-line LangGraph mini-project").

**Minute 17 to 20. Ship the card as a deliverable.**
Drop the card into a file called `segment-agent-engineer.md` at the repo root of whatever portfolio workspace you keep. It will count as your first SHIPPED ARTIFACT, and three progress events will attach, which lifts readiness against the segment from 0% toward the row 53 share of 28%. Row 53 is the entry point the Agent engineer segment demands.
