# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Rasul, using a personal planning dashboard while preparing for senior Forward-Deployed Engineer roles. The dashboard should make a large, multi-month plan feel legible in a short weekly review.

## Product Purpose

Turn the Senior FDE workbook into an actionable command center: see pace, choose the next topic, track mocks, open vetted resources, and keep compensation context close to the plan.

## Brand Personality

Focused, candid, quietly ambitious. Precise enough for planning, human enough to return to every week.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic SaaS analytics wall, decorative charts with no decision value, rainbow status colors, and motivational copy that hides actual workload or trade-offs.

## Design Principles

- Make pace visible before volume.
- Keep the next action close to the evidence.
- Preserve the workbook's nuance while reducing friction.
- Use color to encode state, not decoration.
- Treat resources and reality checks as first-class parts of the plan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible tabs and links, color-independent status labels, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
