## 4. Frequency judgement

**Near-universal (13-15 of 15):** customer discovery; stakeholder communication across technical-to-executive range; scoping and trade-off defence; hands-on production code inside the customer's environment; LLM/agent/eval literacy; a stated travel percentage; ambiguity tolerance and autonomy ("High agency", "thrive under ambiguity", "bias for action", "CEO of the Account"); **codifying repeatable patterns and feeding the field signal back**.

**Common (6-11):** demos, prototypes and POCs; engagement documentation and SOW authoring; vertical/domain fluency; security and compliance posture; production handover; cost and value framing; async written communication; customer enablement.

**Occasional (≤5):** air-gapped and sovereign delivery; clearances and public-sector acquisition; partner/SI orchestration; formal PM ceremonies (sprint reviews, milestone reviews); mentoring and hiring; the EMEA regulatory alphabet (DORA/NIS2/EU AI Act); commercial quota ownership.

**Net read:** the plan's J and K tracks (rows 73-88, 93) are unusually strong on the near-universal set - J-73, J-75, J-80 and K-84/K-87 are more operationally specific than anything the JDs ask for. The exposure is concentrated in one place: the highest-frequency bullet in the corpus (codify patterns → feed Product/Research, 12/15) has the thinnest plan match, and the adjacent pre-sales/adoption motion (GAP-1) is entirely absent while carrying the corpus's top compensation bands.
