# Design

## Direction

An operator's desk at 7:30 AM: cool daylight, one saturated rose marker on a white sheet, dense but calm information, no dashboard theater.

## Palette

Restrained product color with OKLCH tokens. White and cool ink carry the surface; rose marks active focus, brass marks reality checks, and teal marks healthy progress.

```css
--bg: oklch(1 0 0); --surface: oklch(.975 .012 270); --ink: oklch(.22 .025 270);
--muted: oklch(.48 .025 270); --primary: oklch(.52 .19 340); --teal: oklch(.52 .12 180); --brass: oklch(.62 .14 78);
```

## Typography

One system sans stack with a compact product scale. Semibold headings, medium labels, regular body copy.

## Components

Compact top bar, tabs, metric rows, progress bars, responsive table, resource links, and inline filters. Cards group meaningful decisions only, with 12px radii and minimal shadow.
